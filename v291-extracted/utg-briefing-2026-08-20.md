# UTG subcommittee briefing — V2.9.1 Chapter 2C terminology workstream

**2026-08-20.** Prepared for the UTG subcommittee of TSMG.

Companion document: `vocabulary-v2mgmt-items.md`, the full 12-item register.
That register is written for V2 Management and TSMG together. **This briefing
re-cuts it for UTG specifically** — what UTG owns, what UTG needs to decide,
and what UTG should simply know is coming. Items that are purely V2
Management's (document styling, chapter wording) are listed at the end in one
line each and need nothing from this meeting.

---

## 30-second summary

We are building a FHIR IG that carries the V2.9.1 Chapter 2C vocabulary as
CodeSystems and ValueSets. **It is a workspace, not a publication.** Its only
destination is integration into THO, so resources are authored at
`http://terminology.hl7.org/` canonicals from the start.

The extraction and text-fidelity pass is **complete**: 799 tables, 5,540 codes,
every divergence from the published text recorded and reviewed, nothing left
outstanding. Resource generation is the next step and is gated on decisions,
several of which are UTG's.

---

## Decisions we would like from UTG

### 1. Where V2 published an error, does THO reproduce it or correct it?

**This is the one that unblocks the most work, and UTG has already answered it
once by precedent.**

Chapter 2C table **0931 Temperature Units** publishes three codes: `degF`
(status `D`, comment "typo"), `[degF]`, and `Cel`. `degF` is not valid UCUM —
UCUM requires the brackets — so the chapter publishes a known-invalid code
beside its correction, with two of three entries meaning Degrees Fahrenheit.

**THO already fixed this.** `ValueSet/v2-0931` v2.0.0 carries only `[degF]` and
`Cel`, and folds the history into the display text: *"Degrees Fahrenheit (note
that V2 has erroneously published this for many years without the square
brackets)."*

That is a good decision and we would like to know whether it is **policy or a
one-off**. Our working default is the opposite — reproduce and record — and
nobody has ratified it.

> **Ask:** Is "correct in THO, record the history in the display text" the rule?
> If so we will apply it consistently and bring the list of affected tables
> back. If not, 0931 is currently inconsistent with whatever the rule is.

Counter-example worth having in the room: table **0560 Quantity Units** is
named for quantity, bound to `ADJ-6`, and publishes five codes of which four
are units of *time* (`HS` Hours, `DY` Days, `MN` Month, `YY` Years).
**`CodeSystem/v2-0560` in THO 3.0.0 publishes the same five.** So here THO
reproduced rather than corrected. 0931 and 0560 currently point in opposite
directions.

### 2. Merge mechanics — we want a diff that only shows real changes

We will hand UTG a large body of v2 CodeSystems and ValueSets. We would rather
design for a reviewable merge now than retrofit one.

**Settled on our side:** we will emit **XML** into UTG's layout
(`input/sourceOfTruth/v2/{codeSystems,valueSets}/`), and we will match THO's
existing serialization closely enough that **a resource we do not change
produces no diff at all** — no reindentation, no reordered elements, no
whitespace noise. The review should read "here are the N resources that
actually change", not "here are 800 files".

We intend to enforce that with a round-trip test: take THO's existing v2 XML,
run it through our writer untouched, and assert the bytes come back identical.
Anything that fails is a serialization bug, not a content change.

> **Ask, three parts:**
> 1. **Which UTG checkout should we diff against?** We have the published
>    package (`hl7.terminology.r5` v7.3.0, JSON) but not the UTG source repo.
>    We need the repo, and to know which branch is the right base.
> 2. **Are the conventions written down anywhere,** or should we infer them
>    from the existing files? Specifically: element ordering, indent width,
>    self-closing tag style, line endings, and whether the `text` narrative is
>    hand-authored or regenerated by tooling. Narrative is the likeliest source
>    of spurious diffs.
> 3. **What is the intake route** — PR against the UTG repo, a proposal per
>    resource, something else? And what proposal granularity does UTG want for
>    a change set this size?

### 3. `CodeSystem/conceptdomains` — we are proposing 3.0.0 → 4.0.0

Chapter 2C declares **760 distinct concept domains**. THO 3.0.0 publishes 582
of them. We are extending rather than forking:

| | THO 3.0.0 | Proposed 4.0.0 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Top-level concepts | 742 | 919 |
| Total concepts | 1,345 | **1,522** |

The 177 additions are appended as new top-level concepts marked `source: v2` —
THO's own convention for v2-derived domains. Every existing THO concept is
carried through unchanged with its hierarchy and properties, because publishing
a *subset* at THO's canonical would make concepts disappear for anyone loading
both packages. A CodeSystem **supplement** is not an alternative: supplements
may add properties and designations, not concepts.

> **Ask:** Confirm the major-version bump and the `source: v2` marking are what
> UTG wants, and that appending 177 top-level concepts is the right shape
> rather than slotting them into the existing hierarchy.

### 4. Five concept domain definitions where Chapter 2C and THO disagree

**Tables 0952, 0967, 0675, 0066, 0626.** For 577 of the 582 shared domains the
two agree word for word. These five do not, and only 0952 is a near-match
(formatting and a stray space). **The other four are wholly different texts** —
which usually means the two bodies have attached different meanings to the same
domain, not that one meaning is phrased two ways.

Our generator currently emits THO's wording. That was chosen when we assumed
THO was the more reliable side; we now understand the existing THO v2 content
to be less reliable than Chapter 2C in general, so we would rather not have
picked a default by assumption.

> **Ask:** These four need reconciling on the merits, not a tie-break rule.
> Choosing a wording does not fix a genuine disagreement about meaning.

## THO gaps and defects we found — for UTG action

Verified today against `hl7.terminology.r5` v7.3.0.

### 5. `CodeSystem/v2-0411` is referenced by the published chapter and does not exist

Chapter 2C prints the URI `http://terminology.hl7.org/CodeSystem/v2-0411` for
table 0411 *Supplemental Service Information Values* (2 codes: `NDBS`, `CG`;
OID `2.16.840.1.113883.18.452`). **THO has no such resource, and no
`ValueSet/v2-0411` either.** A dangling canonical in a published HL7 standard.

> **Ask:** Should UTG create it, or is there a reason it was never minted?

### 6. THO has no CodeSystem for 18 code-bearing v2 tables — 10 have a ValueSet only

This looks mostly deliberate and correct, and we want to confirm the pattern
before we generalise it across 415 tables.

For **10** of the 18, THO publishes a ValueSet whose `compose` names the real
system, and no CodeSystem — because the codes belong to something else:

| Tables | Codes come from |
|---|---|
| 0567, 0568, 0929, 0930, 0931, 0932 | `http://unitsofmeasure.org` (UCUM) |
| 0725 Mood Codes | `…/CodeSystem/v3-ActMood` |
| 0125 Value Type | `…/CodeSystem/v2-0440` |
| 0136 Yes/no Indicator | `…/CodeSystem/v2-0532` |
| 0338 Practitioner ID Type | `…/CodeSystem/v2-0203` |

In every case THO's enumerated codes match what Chapter 2C prints.

> **Ask:** Confirm the rule — *a v2 table whose codes belong to another system
> gets a ValueSet composing from that system and no CodeSystem of its own.* We
> would apply it across the board, including to the five tables we have already
> resolved this way (0227→`NamingSystem/MVX`, 0347→`NamingSystem/ISO3166Part2`,
> 0929/0930/0932→`NamingSystem/v3-ucum`).

Of the remaining 8: **0820** (LOINC) and **0823** (SNOMED CT + DataAbsentReason
+ NullFlavor) need a ValueSet each and no CodeSystem, same shape. **0460**
*Denial or Rejection Code* is genuinely HL7-authored content with no THO home
and needs a real CodeSystem. **0411** is item 5 above. The last three are item 8
below.

### 7. Table 0459 is labelled as CMS content but does not look like CMS content

Chapter 2C gives table 0459 *Reimbursement Action Code* the symbolic name
`cms-OCEEditCode-cs` and OID `2.16.840.1.113883.6.301.9`. Its four codes are
`0`–`3` with HL7-authored English displays ("OCE line item denial or rejection
is not ignored", …). Those read as an HL7 enumeration *about* OCE behaviour,
not as codes drawn from the CMS OCE Edit Code system.

> **Ask:** Is the OID right? If these are HL7's own codes they need an HL7 code
> system, not a CMS one.

### 8. Three tables declare "Concept Domain Only" and then publish code rows

**0093** *Release Information*, **0340** *Procedure Code Modifier*, **0451**
*Substance Identifier*. Each sets `Concept Domain Only: yes` and then carries
rows in the coded content grid. The rows are not really codes:

- 0093's third entry is literally `...` with display "user-defined codes"
- 0340's two entries (`CPTM`, `HPC`) name *coding systems*, not modifiers
- 0451's single entry `ALL` is a query wildcard

> **Ask:** We plan to emit a concept domain and nothing else for these three.
> Flagging in case UTG reads any of them differently.

## Visibility — no decision needed today

- **We are authoring at THO canonicals.** Resources carry
  `http://terminology.hl7.org/CodeSystem/v2-0nnn` and
  `ValueSet/v2-0nnn` from the start, because the workspace exists only to be
  merged into THO. If anyone would rather we staged under a different base and
  migrated, now is the time to say so — it gets expensive later.
- **The output is not scoped to V2.9.1.** V2.9.1 is the source we are using to
  establish a correct v2 baseline. Resource `version` will not carry `2.9.1`;
  Chapter 2C's "HL7 Version Introduced" becomes a concept property.
- **This pass is fidelity only.** Substantive corrections to the v2 terminology
  are a deliberate second effort. Keeping them separate is what will make the
  second one reviewable against this baseline.
- **Scale.** 799 sections, 5,540 codes, 415 code-bearing tables, ~384
  concept-domain-only, 760 concept domains.
- **Method.** Two independent extractions of the same `.docx` — python-docx
  (character-faithful) and an LLM (structural corroboration) — agreeing on
  740/799 tables, with every remaining difference classified and adjudicated
  against the source. Every change to published text is recorded in
  `vocabulary-changelog.md` with the decision that authorized it.
